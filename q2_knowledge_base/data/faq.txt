NovaFin Business Loan FAQ
Version: 1.0
Status: Training/Prototype Content

Q1. What is the minimum loan amount?
A. The Business Growth Loan has a stated minimum amount of ₹2,00,000.

Q2. What is the maximum loan amount?
A. The stated maximum amount is ₹25,00,000.

Q3. How long can I take the loan for?
A. The available tenure is 12 to 60 months.

Q4. How old must my business be?
A. The business should have at least 24 months of continuous operation under the stated eligibility rule.

Q5. My business is only 18 months old. Can I apply?
A. The stated 24-month business-vintage requirement would not be met. The agent should not promise an exception. If the customer wants clarification about other products, offer human assistance.

Q6. What documents are needed?
A. Typical requirements include identity proof, PAN or applicable tax identification information, business proof/registration where applicable, and recent business bank statements or other requested financial records.

Q7. How quickly can I get an answer?
A. Initial assessment may take around 1–2 business days after the required information is received. This is not a guarantee of final approval.

Q8. What interest rate will I get?
A. Pricing is customer-specific. The agent must not guarantee a rate before the approved pricing process.

Q9. Is collateral required?
A. Security or collateral requirements depend on the application and approved product terms. The agent should not promise that collateral will or will not be required.

Q10. Can I use the loan for inventory?
A. Working capital and inventory purchase are listed as permitted purposes in the product guide, subject to approved terms.

Q11. Can I use it for business expansion?
A. Business expansion is listed as an intended use in the product guide, subject to approved terms.

Q12. I don't have all my documents right now. What should I do?
A. The agent should identify which information is missing and explain that additional documents may be requested during assessment. It should not promise approval without verification.

Q13. Can I get a guaranteed approval?
A. No guarantee should be given. Final approval is subject to verification and internal assessment.

Q14. Can I borrow ₹30 lakh?
A. The Business Growth Loan has a stated maximum of ₹25 lakh. The agent should not promise availability above that amount.

Q15. What if I want to speak to a person?
A. The agent should offer a human callback/escalation path and capture the basic reason for the request.

Q16. Do you have today's interest rates?
A. The prototype knowledge base does not contain a live rate table. The agent should say that current live pricing is unavailable and offer human follow-up rather than inventing a rate.

Q17. What happens after I submit the information?
A. The information is reviewed and may be verified. Additional documents may be requested before a final decision.

Q18. Can I apply if my business is home-based?
A. The current knowledge base does not define a separate home-based-business rule. The agent should avoid inventing one and offer human clarification if necessary.
