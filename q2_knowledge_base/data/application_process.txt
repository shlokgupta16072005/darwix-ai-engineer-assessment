NovaFin Business Loan — Application Process
Version: 1.0

Stage 1 — Initial conversation
Collect only the information needed to understand the customer's request:
- Business type
- Approximate business vintage
- Requested loan amount
- Intended use
- Whether the customer wants a human callback

Stage 2 — Basic eligibility check
Check:
- Business vintage >= 24 months
- Requested amount between ₹2 lakh and ₹25 lakh
- Basic identity/business information is available

Stage 3 — Document collection
Typical information/documents:
- Identity proof
- PAN or applicable tax identification
- Business proof/registration where applicable
- Recent business bank statements or other requested financial records

Stage 4 — Assessment
The information may be reviewed and verified. Additional documents may be requested.

Stage 5 — Decision
Final approval is subject to verification and internal assessment.

Stage 6 — Customer communication
The customer can be informed of the next step. The prototype should not fabricate approval, pricing, disbursement date, or other unavailable information.

Voice-agent decision logic:

IF customer asks for human:
    escalate

ELSE IF requested amount < 200000 OR requested amount > 2500000:
    explain product range and offer human assistance

ELSE IF business vintage < 24 months:
    explain stated eligibility requirement and offer human assistance

ELSE IF required information is incomplete:
    ask clarification

ELSE:
    mark as "preliminary qualification met" and explain that final approval is not guaranteed

Fallback:
If the knowledge base does not contain the answer:
    say that the current information does not provide the answer
    do not guess
    offer human assistance
