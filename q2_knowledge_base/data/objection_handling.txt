NovaFin Business Loan — Objection Handling Guide
Version: 1.0
For prototype conversation design only.

1. Objection: "The interest rate is too high."
Suggested response:
"I understand that the pricing is an important part of the decision. The final pricing is customer-specific and depends on the application and assessment. I can help collect the details needed for an assessment, or arrange a callback if you want to discuss pricing with a representative."

Do not:
- Promise a lower rate.
- Claim a specific rate without approved pricing data.
- Say the customer will definitely be approved.

2. Objection: "I don't want to share my bank statement."
Suggested response:
"I understand the concern. Recent financial records may be requested as part of the assessment. I can explain the information currently listed as required, and if you prefer, I can arrange human assistance to discuss the documentation."

3. Objection: "I don't have all my documents."
Suggested response:
"That's okay for the initial conversation. Let me identify which information you already have and which items may still be required. Additional documents can be requested during the assessment."

4. Objection: "I need the money today."
Suggested response:
"I understand the urgency. The current product guide says initial assessment may take around 1–2 business days after the required information is received, but it does not guarantee same-day approval or disbursement."

5. Objection: "I already told another agent all of this."
Suggested response:
"Sorry for the repetition. I'll keep the questions to the information needed for this assessment and avoid asking for anything that is already available in the current conversation."

6. Objection: "Just tell me if I am approved."
Suggested response:
"I can check the stated eligibility requirements, but I can't guarantee final approval. Final approval depends on verification and internal assessment."

7. Objection: "I don't trust online applications."
Suggested response:
"I understand. I can explain the information needed for the application, and I can also arrange a callback if you prefer to speak with a representative."

8. Objection: "I want a loan of ₹30 lakh."
Suggested response:
"The Business Growth Loan in the current product information has a maximum amount of ₹25 lakh. If you need a larger amount, I can arrange human follow-up to discuss whether another product is available."

9. Objection: "My business is only 18 months old."
Suggested response:
"The current eligibility rule specifies at least 24 months of continuous business operation. Based on that rule, the stated requirement is not met. I can arrange human assistance if you want to ask about other available options."

10. Objection: "I don't want to answer questions."
Suggested response:
"No problem. I can stop the qualification process and arrange a callback or human assistance instead."

Escalation triggers:
- Customer explicitly asks for a human.
- Customer disputes a policy or eligibility decision.
- Customer requests live pricing not present in the knowledge base.
- Customer asks about a product or exception not covered by the knowledge base.
- Customer expresses a complaint that requires human handling.
