# Philippines Localization

## Language

Filipino

## Language Code

`fil`

## Target Market

Philippines

## Localization Approach

The bot uses Filipino for customer-facing interaction rather than translating an English script word-for-word.

## Greeting Style

The greeting is polite, conversational, and suitable for a customer-service interaction.

## Terminology

Common financial terms such as business loan, interest rate, PAN, and bank statement may remain recognizable as standard financial terminology where appropriate.

## Tone

* Polite
* Helpful
* Clear
* Conversational
* Non-promotional

## Safety Behavior

The bot should not invent current interest rates, guarantee approval, or promise a particular loan outcome.

When information is unavailable, it should offer human assistance.

## Example

English intent:
"How long must my business have been operating?"

Localized Filipino response:
"Ang negosyo ay kailangang may hindi bababa sa 24 na buwan ng tuloy-tuloy na operasyon, ayon sa eligibility requirement."
