# Indonesia Localization

## Language

Indonesian

## Language Code

`id`

## Target Market

Indonesia

## Localization Approach

The bot uses Indonesian customer-facing language with natural conversational phrasing rather than performing a literal word-for-word translation.

## Tone

* Polite
* Helpful
* Clear
* Conversational
* Non-promotional

## Terminology

Financial terminology is kept recognizable and appropriate for a business-loan interaction.

## Safety Behavior

The bot must not invent current interest rates, guarantee loan approval, or promise a specific pricing outcome.

If the requested information is unavailable, it should offer human follow-up.

## Example

English intent:
"How long must my business have been operating?"

Localized Indonesian response:
"Usaha harus telah beroperasi secara berkelanjutan selama minimal 24 bulan sesuai dengan persyaratan kelayakan."
